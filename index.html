How I work

How I actually work.

01

Start with what's real

I'd rather watch how people actually behave than argue about it in a doc. The research comes first.

02

Build the whole thing

The plumbing and the part people touch. If those two don't hang together, it doesn't feel trustworthy.

03

Ship, then fix it

Get it live with real data and honest empty states, then make it better. I'd rather ship the truth than a prettier version of it.

How I build with AI

AI is my leverage, not my autopilot.

Most of Arzìqi, and this site, I built by directing AI tools instead of waiting on a full team. The pace looks like a team; the calls, the taste, and the quality bar are mine. I use AI as a force multiplier and stay the person deciding what's actually right.

⚡

Leverage, not autopilot

AI drafts and writes code fast. I own the decisions: exact amounts over abbreviations, cost-basis first, killing the chart that lied. AI is the hands, I'm the head.

🔍

I've judged it from the inside

Evaluating models for Anthropic taught me exactly how they fail: invented APIs, missed instructions, confident nonsense. I know when to trust the output and when not to.

🎯

I know where AI belongs

On Arzìqi, AI is an enhancement layer, not the core promise. The product has to be trustworthy without it. Knowing when not to reach for AI matters as much as knowing when to.

About

I didn't start in tech. My degree's in chemical engineering. I switched, started the PM course at AltSchool Africa, and wanted something real to work on instead of coursework. Arzìqi was that. I joined at the very start, before there was a frontend, and ended up owning most of it: the research, the brand, the design, the build. I even helped hire the engineers.

The thing I keep coming back to is honesty. A product that quietly misleads you does real damage, and money and AI are two places where that really bites. That's what ties the two halves of my work together.
