About

I came into tech from chemical engineering. I started the Product Management track at AltSchool Africa and wanted real 0→1 experience — so I joined Arzìqi at the concept stage, before there was a frontend, and owned it through research, brand, design, and delivery, helping recruit the founding engineering team along the way.

I care about products that are honest with the people using them — especially where the stakes are high. Money and machine intelligence are two places where a system that looks confident but can't be trusted does real damage. That's the thread between the fintech product I build and the AI models I evaluate.
